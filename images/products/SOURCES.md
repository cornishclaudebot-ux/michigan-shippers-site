# Product photo sources

Every product photo in the supply store traces to the manufacturer's own website or
official datasheet. Michigan Shippers Supply is an authorized wholesale distributor for
each of these brands, and these are the brands' own product photography, used to
represent products we resell.

Captured 2026-08-03. Tiles are background-removed, squared, and saved as WebP at
700x700, roughly 30 KB each (the PNG placeholders they replaced were about 500 KB each).

## Deliberately excluded, and why

- Any `shutterstock_*` asset found on a manufacturer site. Those are stock photos the
  manufacturer licensed for its OWN use; a distributor permission does not extend to them.

- Avery Dennison application photos showing third-party BRANDED end products (kombucha,
  wine, olive oil, cosmetics). Publishing those would imply those companies are Michigan
  Shippers customers, which is not true. The one Avery asset used is a bare application
  TOOL with no third-party branding in frame.

- SATO's 'Eco-Savvy Wipes' sachet: the packaging carries no SATO branding at all and
  actually reads as a different cleaning brand. Used the SATO-branded E-Z Wipes concept
  instead where needed.

- SATO's M84Pro printhead composite (marketing graphic with baked-in callout arrows) and
  DNP `ribbon_12` (shot on a concrete floor rather than a studio backdrop).

## The 14 SKUs still on inline SVG art, and why

No official photo exists for these from any of the eight authorized brands, so they keep
the hand-made line art in `product-art.js` (zero HTTP requests). Do NOT substitute
reseller, marketplace, or lookalike photos.

- `uv-gloss`, `uv-matte`, `spot-uv` - liquid UV varnish and spot-UV coating are made by
  print-chemistry companies (ACTEGA, Flint, Sun Chemical, Zeller+Gmelin), none of them an
  authorized brand. UPM and Avery reference 'varnish' only as a coating already applied to
  their facestock, not as a sellable liquid.
- `bc-verify` - a service. Standalone verifier hardware is made by Axicon, Webscan,
  Omron/Microscan and REA; Honeywell's Quick Check is discontinued and Zebra sells no verifier.
- `cd-makeup`, `cd-cartridge` - continuous-inkjet solvent and TIJ coding cartridges from
  Videojet / Domino / Markem-Imaje. U-Mark makes solid-paint markers, not coding consumables.
- `sw-bartender-start`, `sw-bartender-pro`, `sw-nicelabel`, `sw-training` - third-party
  software and a training service.
- `cu-quote`, `cu-managed`, `cu-die`, `cu-rush` - services. Nothing to photograph.

## Notes worth keeping

- The Zebra GK420d is DISCONTINUED (sales ended January 2022; successor is the ZD421). The
  photo is genuine and still hosted by Zebra, and the SKU sells it as a refurbished spare,
  which is legitimate. Just do not describe it as a current model.
- The printhead photo is Zebra part P1004238, a genuine 203 dpi printhead for the Xi4
  industrial series (110Xi4 / 170Xi4 / 220Xi4). Do NOT call it a ZM400 part in copy.
- `pm-12pack` was retitled to "Paint Markers, White (12-pack)": U-Mark makes no ASSORTED
  12-pack, so the old "Assorted / Mixed colors" copy matched no real product.
- `app-hand` is a SATO hand labeler. Auto Labe's line is benchtop-only, so this SKU is
  correctly sourced from SATO, not Auto Labe.
- `sato-cl4sxr` is named from SATO's own image alt text ("CL4-SXR"), not from a filename.
